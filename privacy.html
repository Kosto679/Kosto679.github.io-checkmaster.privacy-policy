CheckMaster Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 July 2026
App: CheckMaster (com.checkmaster.app)
Contact: support@checkmaster.app

This page describes how the CheckMaster mobile app handles information. It is provided so you can publish a Play Store privacy policy URL. Have it reviewed if you need formal legal advice for your jurisdiction.

1. Who we are

CheckMaster (“we”, “us”) is a checklist and peer-review app. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the Android app and related backend services.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account data. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, we receive the identifiers Google provides for sign-in (such as a Google account ID and display name). We use this to keep your data across devices.
  • App content you create. Checklist titles and items, completion status, streaks and badges, share/join codes, peer-review submissions, review decisions, and optional comments.
  • Photo proofs. If a task requires a photo, images you attach may be stored and shown to the checklist owner (and related review history) so they can verify the work. Do not upload photos of other people without their permission.
  • Device / advertising identifiers. On free (non‑Pro) use, Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and similar signals to show ads and measure performance, subject to your consent choices where required (for example in the EEA/UK).
  • Local preferences. Language, onboarding flags, and offline caches may be stored on your device (for example in local storage) so the app works when you are offline.
  • Notifications. If you opt in, the app may schedule local reminders (such as a streak reminder) on your device. These are not used for advertising.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and sync checklists, peer review, and history
  • Authenticate you (guest or Google) and protect account data
  • Show ads on the free tier and honour consent / privacy options
  • Maintain optional local reminders you enable
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse (for example invalid share use)

4. Sharing

  • Peer review. When you join someone’s checklist or submit work for review, the checklist owner can see the relevant submission (including photo proofs and your display name).
  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) to run the backend, and Google AdMob for advertising on the free tier. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and consent

Free-tier users may see banner and rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. In regions that require it, we show a consent form (UMP) before personalised ads are requested. You can open ad privacy options from the in-app profile when Google requires that entry point. Pro features, when offered with a real purchase flow in a future version, are intended to remove ads; the current public release may not offer paid upgrades yet.

6. Data retention

Account and app content remain while your account and related documents exist in our Firebase project. Anonymous accounts are tied to the app install; signing in with Google is how you keep data if you change devices. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us at the email above (include enough detail for us to identify the account). Device-local caches are cleared when you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

7. Security

We use Google Firebase authentication and security rules to restrict access to your data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; use the app accordingly.

8. Children

CheckMaster is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International processing

Firebase and AdMob may process data in the United States or other countries where Google operates. By using the app you understand that your information may be processed outside your home country, subject to applicable safeguards and your consent choices for ads.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
