Delete your CheckMaster account

App: CheckMaster (com.checkmaster.app)
Use this page to request deletion of your app account and associated data. No sign-in is required.

What gets deleted

When we process your request, we delete:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (guest or Google-linked)
  • Your user profile and gamification data (streaks, badges, Pro flag)
  • Checklists you own and their items
  • Peer reviews and audit history tied to your account
  • Share/invitation codes you created

Photo proofs that were part of a review you submitted to someone else may already appear in their history; we remove what we control on your account and related documents. Data held only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

How to request deletion

Option A — Email (recommended)

Send a message from the Google account you use with CheckMaster (or state your display name / approximate signup time if you only used guest mode):

Email deletion request

To: nikoskkostopoulos@gmail.com
Subject: CheckMaster account deletion request

Option B — Also revoke Google access

If you signed in with Google, you can additionally revoke CheckMaster’s access in your Google Account:

Google Account → Third-party access

Revoking Google access alone does not delete Firestore data; still send the email above so we can wipe your app account.

Timeline

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of a verifiable request. You will get a short confirmation email when it is done. We do not “freeze” accounts — deletion removes the account and associated app data, except where a short retention is required for security or legal reasons (we will say so if that applies).
